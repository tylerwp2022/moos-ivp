# Documentation standards

## Organize by reader need

This handbook uses [Diátaxis](https://diataxis.fr/start-here/). Tutorials guide a learning experience. How-to guides solve a task. Reference pages describe exact interfaces. Explanation pages give concepts and rationale.

Audience routes point to shared pages. Component manuals retain their component contracts. Decisions and dated evidence are separate from current procedures.

## Write with ASD-STE100 guidance

The author consulted the official [ASD-STE100 FAQ](https://www.asd-ste100.org/STE_faq.html) and indexed extracts of [Issue 9](https://www.asd-ste100.org/assets/files/ASD-STE100_ISSUE9.pdf), including procedural writing rules and approved-word guidance.

**Access limitation, November 20, 2026:** direct retrieval of the full standard returned HTTP 403. The full writing rules and dictionary were not available for a complete review. This handbook does not claim full ASD-STE100 compliance.

The editorial review uses direct instructions, active voice, consistent terms, and conditions before dependent actions. Procedures aim for one instruction per sentence and the standard's short-sentence guidance. Exact identifiers and executable examples are preserved.

Approved vocabulary depends on meaning and part of speech. Short sentences or a word checker cannot establish compliance. The [terminology list](../reference/terminology.md) records project terms for subsequent review against the complete standard.

Obtain an official copy through the [ASD-STE100 download page](https://www.asd-ste100.org/STE_downloads.html) before a full compliance review. Do not silently turn a limited editorial pass into a compliance claim.

## Keep claims tied to source

Link exact source revisions and the relevant checks. State whether an example uses a simulation, fixture, replay, or real service. Retain original dates and claims in historical records.

A prose edit does not change a runtime verification date. Screenshots must identify the version, inputs, settings, and observation they show. Conceptual diagrams must be labeled as such and include a text equivalent.

## Maintain the handbook

Build with strict warnings. Validate local links, anchors, navigation, and assets. Inspect rendered pages, including narrow screens and search. The [publication guide](../how-to/publish-site.md) gives the commands and the required Pages setup.

Use `Acting agent: Cline/ChatGPT` in project work histories. Record the actual human owner separately. Routine prose edits do not need their own work journal.
