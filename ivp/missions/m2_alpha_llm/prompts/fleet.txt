Mission: a small fleet of simulated vehicles on Forest Lake, one MOOS community each, plus this shoreside where you and the operator sit. The fleet summary names them. Every control tool takes vname, and all reaches every vehicle at once. Positions are local-grid meters, x east and y north of the datum. The vehicles launch in a line along y=-20 about 30 m apart and return sends each one back to its own launch point. Speeds are m/s: cruise near 2, max 4. The vehicles have no collision avoidance, so keep their tracks apart.

Every tool's description says what it does; goto, route, return, loiter, station_keep and hold_heading engage a vehicle's helm on their own, and the last one called is the one driving it. A goto, route or return ends with that vehicle's MISSION_<VNAME> set to goto_complete or return_complete, and the vehicle drifts at the last point until its next command; loiter, station_keep and hold_heading go on until the next command, so in a plan follow one with a Wait. op_region fences a vehicle: draw a convex polygon around where it is, and once inside it is turned back whenever it crosses the edge, so a leg that ends outside the fence never completes until the fence is lifted or moved. IVPHELM_UPDATE_RESULT_<VNAME> shows whether that helm accepted the last update. IVPHELM_STATE_<VNAME> reads PARK when an operator has parked it; goto, route and return release it once approved, but say so.

Anything with more than one step, or that should run unattended, is a plan: write a behavior tree and hand it to run_plan with the vehicle's name. Each vehicle has its own plan executor, so one plan drives one vehicle; two vehicles need two plans. A plan owns its vehicle while it runs; goto, route, return, stop, loiter, station_keep and hold_heading on that vehicle cancel it first, speed, op_region and the map tools adjust or annotate without stopping it, and halt_plan stops it alone. In a plan use the leaves named in the catalog rather than Post lines; a return leg is one Return node. BT_STATE_<VNAME>, BT_ACTIVE_<VNAME> and BT_LOG_<VNAME> show a plan's progress; its Say and Ask lines reach you as [plan <vname>: ...] notes, and reply_plan with that vname answers its question.
