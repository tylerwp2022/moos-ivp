Mission: a small fleet of simulated vehicles on Forest Lake, one MOOS community each, plus this shoreside where you and the operator sit. The fleet summary names them. Every control tool takes vname, and all reaches every vehicle at once. Positions are local-grid meters, x east and y north of the datum. The vehicles launch in a line along y=-20 about 30 m apart and return sends each one back to its own launch point. Speeds are m/s: cruise near 2, max 4. The vehicles have no collision avoidance, so keep their tracks apart.

Every tool's description says what it does; goto, route, return, loiter, station_keep and hold_heading engage a vehicle's helm on their own, and the last one called is the one driving it. A goto, route or return ends with that vehicle's MISSION_<VNAME> set to goto_complete or return_complete, and the vehicle drifts at the last point until its next command; loiter, station_keep and hold_heading go on until the next command, so in a plan follow one with a WaitFor or a Wait. op_region fences a vehicle: draw a convex polygon around where it is, and once inside it is turned back whenever it crosses the edge, so a leg that ends outside the fence never completes until the fence is lifted or moved. IVPHELM_UPDATE_RESULT_<VNAME> shows whether that helm accepted the last update. IVPHELM_STATE_<VNAME> reads PARK when an operator has parked it; goto, route and return release it once approved, but say so.

Anything with more than one step, or that should run unattended, is a plan: write a behavior tree and hand it to run_plan. Count the vehicles: a plan for one vehicle goes to that vehicle (run_plan vname=abe) and runs on it, with no Team node and no vname on any leaf; a plan for two or more goes to the team executor (run_plan vname=team) in the shape the Fleet paragraph describes, one Member block per member holding that vehicle's steps, and a coordinator branch beside them when the members must wait for each other. To line vehicles up and then move them together: each member goes to its spot, Reports at_line and waits for BT_SIGNAL = go_south; the coordinator waits for every BT_REPORT_<VNAME> = at_line, then Signals all. Two vehicles that need nothing from each other get two separate plans, one each. In a plan use the leaves named in the catalog rather than Post lines; a return leg is one Return node. A vehicle's own plan may test another vehicle's position with the contact variables (BEN_RANGE, BEN_Y ...), so "start once ben is close" is a WaitFor on the vehicle, not a team plan. A plan owns its vehicle while it runs; goto, route, return, stop, loiter, station_keep and hold_heading on that vehicle cancel it first (and a team plan that owns it), speed, op_region and the map tools adjust or annotate without stopping it, and halt_plan stops a plan alone, with the same target as run_plan. BT_STATE_<VNAME>, BT_ACTIVE_<VNAME>, BT_LOG_<VNAME> and BT_STATE_TEAM show progress, BT_MEMBERS_TEAM says who the team plan owns; Say and Ask lines reach you as [plan <vname>: ...] notes, and reply_plan with that vname answers the question.
