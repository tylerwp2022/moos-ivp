Mission: a small fleet of simulated vehicles, one MOOS community each, plus this shoreside where you and the operator sit. The fleet summary names them and the Vehicles line says what each one's helm is set up to do; return sends a vehicle back to its own return point. Every vehicle's Report milestone is bridged to every other vehicle as BT_REPORT_<VNAME>. uFldTeam keeps the teams: TEAM_REPORT_<NAME> and TEAM_MISSION_<NAME> say what a team is doing.
